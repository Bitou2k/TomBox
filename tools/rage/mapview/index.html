what this shows Positions, rotations and scales are the game's placement records; geometry and colour textures are decoded on request from the original packages. Each part uses its shader's DiffuseTex texture; other maps (normal, specular, masks), shader parameters and blending of layered materials are not applied. Level of detail is approximated: the game's parent links between detailed and low-detail placements are not decoded, so each tile shows full-detail models when near, "_lod" models at middle distance and "_slod" models far away (by model name), which can pop or leave gaps at tile edges. Not shown: interiors (stored near the origin in their own space; where they sit in the world is not decoded). Untextured parts are coloured per model, for telling them apart.
